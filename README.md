### AI is becoming more capable
 
**I keep wondering whether people will become more capable with it**
 
</div>
 
<br>
 
I care about the human side of AI: what people understand, what they carry forward and whether they still have room to judge for themselves.
 
Knowledge reuse asks what people can carry from one piece of work into the next. Accessibility asks what a system quietly demands before someone can take part. I am interested in technology that makes room for people, rather than asking people to fit themselves around it.
 
When AI joins the process, its evidence and limits should remain visible. People should be able to question an answer, change course and decide what is worth carrying forward.
 
My work in sustainability has made me wary of separating value from the way it is created. I bring the same instinct to AI: what becomes possible matters, and so does what happens to people along the way.
 
These are some of the questions behind what I build here.
